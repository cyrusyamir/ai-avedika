## About the Project

We are hiring Online Survey Respondents for an ongoing project involving UK and European consumer finance surveys.

Selected freelancers will be responsible for completing online surveys according to the project's instructions and quality requirements. This is a fully remote, project-based opportunity suitable for students, freelancers, part-time workers, and individuals looking for flexible online work.

Prior survey or data-related experience is not required. Selected candidates will receive training material explaining the survey process, workflow, tools, and quality requirements.

## Key Responsibilities

- Complete assigned online consumer finance surveys.
- Carefully read and follow the provided project instructions.
- Submit surveys accurately and according to project guidelines.
- Maintain consistent quality across survey submissions.
- Meet reasonable daily productivity targets based on your availability.
- Use required browser tools, extensions, VPNs, or project software when instructed and permitted.
- Report technical problems or survey-related issues to the project team.
- Follow updates to project guidelines and workflows.
- Maintain the confidentiality of project information and materials.

## Requirements

Candidates should have:

- A laptop, desktop, smartphone, or other compatible device.
- A stable and reliable internet connection.
- Basic knowledge of using websites and web browsers.
- Ability to understand and follow written instructions accurately.
- Ability to learn a simple online workflow through video training.
- Good attention to detail.
- Ability to work independently and manage assigned tasks.
- Availability to complete surveys consistently during the project.
- Basic familiarity with VPNs, browser extensions, online surveys, or similar tools is helpful but not mandatory.

## Preferred Candidates

Preference may be given to applicants who:

- Have access to a laptop or desktop computer.
- Can complete 50+ surveys per day, subject to survey availability and project requirements.
- Can start immediately or within a few days.
- Have previous experience with online surveys, data annotation, transcription, data collection, AI training, research, or similar freelance projects.
- Are comfortable learning and using project-specific online tools.
- Have already reviewed the provided training material.

Previous experience is an advantage but not a requirement.

## Work Schedule

This is a flexible, project-based freelance opportunity.

You may work according to your availability while meeting project requirements and deadlines. Candidates who can consistently dedicate 2–6 hours per day are preferred.

Survey availability and workload may vary during the project, so a specific number of surveys per day is not guaranteed.

## Payment

Rate: ₹30 per successfully completed and approved survey.

Payment is based on surveys that meet the project's completion and quality requirements.

Incomplete, duplicate, invalid, rejected, or non-compliant submissions may not qualify for payment.

Payments will be processed according to the communicated project payment schedule.

## Training & Onboarding

Shortlisted applicants will receive training and onboarding instructions covering:

- Survey workflow
- Tools and technical setup
- Step-by-step survey completion process
- Quality requirements
- Submission procedures
- Common mistakes to avoid
- Payment and reporting process

Applicants should review the training carefully before beginning production work.

## Confidentiality & Quality

All freelancers are expected to maintain the confidentiality of project-related information.

Project credentials, internal instructions, answer sheets, training materials, datasets, or other confidential resources must not be shared with unauthorized individuals.

Participants must follow the survey platform's rules and the project's instructions. Any activity involving fabricated information, duplicate submissions, unauthorized automation, or attempts to circumvent survey eligibility or platform controls may result in rejection of work and removal from the project.

## Who Can Apply?

You may apply if you are:

- Based in India
- 18 years or older
- A student, freelancer, working professional, or part-time worker
- Comfortable working remotely
- Able to follow detailed instructions
- Interested in flexible project-based work

No formal degree or previous professional experience is required.

## Selection Process

Application → Screening → Training → Qualification/Review → Project Onboarding → Start Work

Only shortlisted applicants will receive further onboarding and project access details via email or WhatsApp.

Note: This is a freelance/project-based opportunity and does not constitute permanent employment. Work volume and survey availability may vary throughout the project.
